# Data use and provenance notice

The research files in this folder are derived principally from public U.S. Energy Information Administration SEDS and USAspending data. Users should comply with the terms and citation guidance of the original data providers.

The repository also documents related published research and public replication materials by Gilmore and St.Clair. Their papers should be cited when their materials or measurement ideas are used. No ownership claim is made over third-party source data or publications.

The derived variables and analytical organization are supplied for scholarly transparency and reproducibility. Users extending the data beyond the release period should verify source revisions directly with the original providers.
