About me

Finance research with measurable policy and sustainability relevance

I connect finance, institutions and technology to environmental and economic outcomes, with particular interest in emerging and developing economies.

I am an Assistant Professor at Multimedia University, Malaysia. I previously served as an Associate Professor in Finance at Sir Padampat Singhania University and as an International Visiting Faculty Member at IIM Ranchi. I also maintain international research and visiting affiliations.

Before entering academia, I worked in banking and accounting in Ghana, including branch management, business development and credit leadership.

I hold a PhD in Business Administration (Finance), an MBA in Banking and Finance, a BBA in Accounting and an HND in Accounting. I am a Certified Practising Accountant (CPA Australia) and have completed UN CC:Learn training in sustainable finance.

My research agenda

What I work on

My research connects financial systems with energy transition, environmental performance, institutional quality and emerging technology.

Climate & Sustainable Finance

I study green finance, climate-finance allocation, sustainable investment, environmental reporting, green bonds and the conversion of finance into real outcomes.

Financial & Energy Economics

I examine financial development, renewable energy, energy transition, environmental taxation, economic growth and sustainability.

Markets, AI & Uncertainty

I work on AI-enabled finance, sovereign risk, uncertainty transmission, connectedness, forecasting and machine-learning applications.

Flagship research infrastructure

Malaysia Climate Finance Observatory

I am developing the Observatory as a project-level evidence platform to trace how climate finance is mobilised, allocated and converted into measurable economic and environmental outcomes in Malaysia.

2015–2026

I focus on Malaysia’s modern green-finance architecture, green sukuk market and traceable project-level instruments.

Finance channels

I track GTFS/MGTC-linked finance, SRI sukuk and bonds, public and development-bank finance and identifiable project-finance transactions.

Outcome channels

I link financial flows to renewable-energy deployment, GHG outcomes, implementation evidence, adaptation and flood-resilience investment.
